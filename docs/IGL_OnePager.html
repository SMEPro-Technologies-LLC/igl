IGL

Identity-Governed Logic

A way to build artificial intelligence that has to answer for what it does.
Who is acting is known
The limits are enforced
What happened is proven

IGL is a programming language for building AI that stays accountable. When an AI is built with IGL, everything it does is tied to a real, named person or system that is allowed to do it, kept inside limits set in advance, and written down in a form anyone can check afterward. Ordinary AI does none of this: it answers whoever asks and leaves nothing you can verify. IGL is the piece that makes an AI show who it works for, stay inside the lines, and prove what it did.

How it works, at a glance
Ordinary AI takes a request and hands back an answer with nothing behind it. IGL routes the same request through three plain questions before it lets anything out, so the result comes with proof.

What it changes for you

If you run a business

Company, regulated industry, operator
  • Use AI where you could not before. In banking, energy, healthcare, and law, "we think it is safe" does not pass. IGL gives proof, so AI can take on work compliance would block.
  • Every decision brings its own paper trail. You see which rules were followed and who signed off, with no scramble after something breaks.
  • Mistakes stay small. Permissions never grow on their own, and one wrong call does not spread through the system.
  • You can prove it to outsiders. Auditors, regulators, insurers, and partners can check the AI for themselves.
In short: AI you can safely put to work on serious jobs.

If you are one person

Professional, customer, citizen
  • The AI answers to you. When it decides something about you, a named authority stands behind it and leaves a record you can see.
  • It cannot make things up about you. It can point to real information, but not invent a fact and pass it off as true.
  • Your permissions stay put. They change only when someone approves it on purpose, not because a system picked up a habit.
  • You have somewhere to turn when it is wrong. The record shows exactly what happened and why.
In short: AI that owes you an explanation, and can give one.
Ordinary AI asks you to trust that it behaved. AI built on IGL lets you prove that it did. Every action is tied to who allowed it, held inside limits it cannot cross, and saved in a record anyone can check. That is the whole idea.
