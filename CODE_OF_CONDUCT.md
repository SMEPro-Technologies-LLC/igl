# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, in substance: be
respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the work, and do not harass,
demean, or discriminate. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, and
contributors that violate this standard.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment of any kind, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of
discussion. Reports are reviewed by the maintainers, who will protect the
privacy of reporters.

Report conduct issues to supprt@smeprotech.com. For security vulnerabilities, use
SECURITY.md instead, not the conduct address.
